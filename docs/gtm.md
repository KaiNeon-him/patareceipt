## Pricing (KES)
| Tier | Price (KES) | Price (USD) | Features |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Free | KSh 0 | $0 | Up to 10 receipts/month, PataReceipt watermark |
| Pro | KSh 499/month | ~$3.85 | Unlimited receipts, custom branding, no watermark, priority support |
| Business | KSh 1,499/month | ~$11.60 | Multi-user accounts, API access, custom templates, bulk generation |

**Rationale:** KSh 499/month is below the KSh 500,000/year KRA tax threshold for most informal sellers, keeps the price psychologically under KSh 500, and aligns with part-time gig worker earnings (KSh 15,000–50,000/month). M-Pesa is the default payment method — card penetration is low, mobile money is universal.

## Phase 1 — Beta Launch (Months 1–3)
**Target:** 100–200 beta users in Nairobi
**Channels:**
- Freelancer Facebook groups, Twitter/X communities, WhatsApp business groups in Nairobi
- Manual onboarding: founder personally sets up each beta user
- Direct outreach to social media sellers (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp vendors)
**Goals:**
- Product refinement
- Testimonial collection
- 20+ dispute cases resolved using PataReceipt receipts (social proof)
**Success metric:** 30% week-over-week retention

## Phase 2 — Soft Launch: Kenya (Months 4–6)
**Target:** 1,000 active users in Kenya
**Channels:**
- WhatsApp Community marketing (topic-based communities for sellers)
- Micro-influencers: successful freelancers showing "Secured by PataReceipt" receipts
- Partnerships with freelancer training programs and digital skills bootcamps
- Instagram/Facebook/TikTok ads targeting "freelancer Kenya," "social media seller"
- Referral program: 1 month free Pro for each friend who signs up
- M-Pesa Till/Pochi integration for Pro/Business subscription payments
**Goals:**
- Validate product-market fit
- 5% conversion to paid tier
- KSh 24,950 monthly recurring revenue at 50 Pro users
**Success metric:** 1,000 MAU, 5% paid conversion

## Phase 3 — Scale: East Africa (Months 7–12)
**Target:** 10,000+ users across East Africa
**Channels:**
- Content marketing: "How to protect yourself from refund scams" blog/social series
- M-Pesa integration partnerships (Phase 3 revenue stream — 1–2% transaction fee)
- Offline activations at freelancer co-working spaces, university entrepreneurship clubs
- Expand to Uganda, Tanzania
- Local language support (Swahili)
**Goals:**
- 10,000+ users
- 8–10% paid conversion
- Path to break-even (~18 months post-launch)

## Viral Loop
Every receipt sent to a client includes "Secured by PataReceipt" watermark → clients who are also sellers discover the app organically → sign up → send their own receipts.

## Key Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low willingness to pay KSh 499/mo | Introduce weekly/daily pricing tiers (KSh 50/week); M-Pesa micropayments |
| WhatsApp view tracking limitations | Use hosted verification links with QR codes on receipts |
| High CAC | Lean on organic WhatsApp communities and referral loop before paid ads |
| Regulatory compliance (Data Protection Act 2019) | Engage legal counsel early; privacy-first design already in product |
| M-Pesa transaction fees eroding margins | Negotiate Safaricom business API rates; bundle into subscription |
| Currency volatility (KES/USD) | Invoice in KES locally; hedge only for cross-border Phase 3 |
